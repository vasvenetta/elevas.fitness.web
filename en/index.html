Your month, from the inside

Each phase asks for something different. The plan knows.

Your energy and your recovery are not the same all your life or all month long. Instead of asking the same of you every day, ELEVAS adjusts the demand to the phase you are in.

Menstrual
No pressure

Same routine, less demand. If one day you do not hit the reps, nothing happens. The goal is to move, not to force it.

Follicular and ovulation
Go for it

The window to push up. You add load or reps and, on ovulation days, you go for your records with strict technique.

Early luteal
Keep pushing

You still have energy. You keep or beat last week's loads, with more hydration and more rest.

Late luteal
Easy week

The seven days before your period. Technique and comfortable loads, and if your body asks you to ease off, you cut sets without guilt.

Not a one-off month

A master plan for a whole year

Each cycle you open a new month, with different exercises, inside one plan that moves forward from start to finish. The goal of the glute development plan (and of the rest of the body) is to help you build a top physique, and you can also choose maintenance or fat loss. How you look will depend only on your consistency, the plan you choose and your nutrition.

BLOCK 1

Foundations and technique

You build foundations and learn to execute safely. High ranges and control before weight.

BLOCK 2

Strength

You raise the loads on the big lifts. Your body gets used to moving more weight with good form.

BLOCK 3

Hypertrophy

Maximum volume to grow. This is the stage where the muscle responds and starts to show.

BLOCK 4

Definition

Heavy basics and more isolation to polish the year's work and bring out the shape.

Your month does not end at 4 weeks. Each new month starts 7 days before your expected period: the two easy weeks come first and the two strong weeks close the month. We adapt to you, not the other way round.

Your whole life counts

A woman's body changes. So does your plan.

The cycle is not the only thing that moves. ELEVAS adapts to every moment you go through, without leaving you alone in any of them.

Pregnancy and postpartum

Adjustments by trimester and a gradual return after birth, always as guidance and with the reminder to train with your doctor's or midwife's approval.

Perimenopause and menopause

At this stage, strength is your best tool to look after muscle and bone. Steady guidance, with no cycle phases, designed to keep you consistent.

With an injury

If you have a sensitive area, the plan avoids the exercises that load it and fills the day working around it, so you stay strong while you recover.

Our commitment

We were born because we got tired of training with plans designed for other bodies.

For years, strength training has been written for men. The same routines for everyone, ignoring that a woman's body has its own rhythm. ELEVAS comes from there: from wanting to train respecting the cycle instead of fighting it.
