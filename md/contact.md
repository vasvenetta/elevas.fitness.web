# Contact

> One address for everything: questions, subscriptions and your data.

## Email

Write to [contacto@elevas-fitness.com](mailto:contacto@elevas-fitness.com). We reply by email, in Spanish or English. If your question is about your account or your subscription, write from the email address you registered with.

## Your subscription

You manage your plan from your account in the app: you can change it or cancel it at any time, keeping access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Payments are handled by Stripe; ELEVAS never sees your card details.

## Your data

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, deletion, objection, restriction and portability by writing to the same address from the email you registered with. We answer within one month at most. If you delete your account from your profile, all your data is removed immediately. The full details are in the [privacy summary](https://elevas-fitness.com/privacy/) and in the [privacy policy](https://elevas-fitness.com/politica-de-privacidad/), in Spanish.

## Legal details

The identity, tax number and address of the owner of ELEVAS are published in the [legal notice](https://elevas-fitness.com/aviso-legal/), in Spanish.
