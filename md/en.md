# ELEVAS

> Train with your cycle, not against it.

A strength plan that adjusts its guidance to each phase of the month and changes with each stage of your life. Your body sets the pace and your training follows. Create your account and get your first plan in two minutes.

## Each phase asks for something different. The plan knows.

Your energy and your recovery are not the same all your life or all month long. Instead of asking the same of you every day, ELEVAS adjusts the demand to the phase you are in.

- **Menstrual. No pressure.** Same routine, less demand. If one day you do not hit the reps, nothing happens. The goal is to move, not to force it.
- **Follicular and ovulation. Go for it.** The window to push up. You add load or reps and, on ovulation days, you go for your records with strict technique.
- **Early luteal. Keep pushing.** You still have energy. You keep or beat last week's loads, with more hydration and more rest.
- **Late luteal. Easy week.** The seven days before your period. Technique and comfortable loads, and if your body asks you to ease off, you cut sets without guilt.

## Machine taken? An injury? You keep training.

- **Machine taken.** We give you the alternative right away, so you do not skip your workout because of it. Every exercise comes with its alternative: you swap on the spot and carry on. Included in Premium.
- **With an injury or a niggle.** If you have a sensitive area, the plan avoids the exercises that load it and fills the day working around it, so you stay strong while you recover. Included in Premium.

## A master plan for a whole year

Each cycle you open a new month, with different exercises, inside one plan that moves forward from start to finish, in four blocks: foundations and technique, strength, hypertrophy and definition. Your month does not end at 4 weeks: each new month starts 7 days before your expected period, so the two easy weeks come first and the two strong weeks close the month.

## A woman's body changes. So does your plan.

- **Pregnancy and postpartum.** Adjustments by trimester and a gradual return after birth, always as guidance and with the reminder to train with your doctor's or midwife's approval.
- **Perimenopause and menopause.** Strength is your best tool to look after muscle and bone. Steady guidance, with no cycle phases.
- **With an injury.** The plan avoids the exercises that load the sensitive area and fills the day working around it.

## See your progress phase by phase

Every weight you log is saved and sorted by cycle. So you see how you evolve within the month, comparing the same squat in menstrual, follicular and ovulation, early luteal and late luteal.

## Frequently asked questions

- **Do I have to train differently in each phase of my cycle?** Keep it simple. Your exercises for the week are the same; what changes is the guidance, small pointers on how to approach each session depending on how your body usually feels those days.
- **How does the app know where I am in my cycle?** You log your periods in the app and it works out your phases from your latest cycles. It is an estimate: if your period comes early or late, it readjusts with the next entry. If your cycle is irregular, your month lasts a fixed 28 days and the phases are shown only as guidance.
- **What if I am in menopause, pregnant or postpartum?** It is for you too. The training adapts to your stage, always with the reminder to train with your doctor's approval.
- **Do I need previous gym experience?** No. If you are starting from scratch or coming from another sport, your first weeks focus on learning technique calmly before raising loads.

ELEVAS is not a medical service. Check with your doctor before starting any training programme.
